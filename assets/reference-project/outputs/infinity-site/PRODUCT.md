# APERTURE

A fictional AI knowledge workspace, presented as a local interactive design demonstration. The audience includes visitors exploring the sculpture and viewers learning the Blender-to-browser workflow.

The primary action is to explore three prepared notebook answers and follow their original source notes. There is no live AI backend, sign-up, data collection, or commercial performance claim.

The user approved a charcoal and acid-yellow visual world with a machined infinity sculpture. Preserve the desktop hover, drag, expansion and pulsing light interactions. Use still imagery and normal document flow for coarse-pointer or reduced-motion visitors. Keep the page accessible by keyboard, and preserve a usable fallback when graphics fail.

Approved surface composition lives in ../landing-build/direction.md and ../landing-page-mockups/. The implementation runs locally on port 4175.
